Subly - Live AI Subtitles for Any Video

Add real-time subtitles to any video playing in your browser. Subly captures tab audio and turns speech into subtitles with AI. Run it fully on your device for free, or pick a cloud model for higher accuracy with your own API key.

FEATURES

- Live subtitles for any HTML5 video on any website
- Local mode: OpenAI's Whisper runs directly in your browser, your audio never leaves your device
- Cloud mode: Groq, OpenAI, Deepgram or Google Gemini with your own API key (Groq, Deepgram and Gemini have free tiers)
- 22 video languages, including English, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, French, German, Japanese, Turkish, Finnish and Swedish
- Translate subtitles into any of the 22 languages
- Customizable font size and subtitle position
- Works in fullscreen mode
- Four local models: Tiny (fastest), Base (balanced), Small, and Large v3 Turbo (best quality)
- WebGPU accelerated for near real-time performance

HOW IT WORKS

1. Play any video in your browser
2. Click the Subly icon and choose the video language, subtitle mode and model
3. Click "Start subtitles" and subtitles appear within seconds
4. Click "Stop subtitles" when you're done

PRIVACY

With a local model, all speech recognition runs on your device using WebGPU/WASM and no audio is uploaded. The model is downloaded once (about 41-760 MB) and cached in your browser.

With a cloud model, short audio clips are sent only to the provider you choose, using your own API key.

Most translations send the transcribed text, not audio, to Google Translate. Translating to English with Whisper models, Groq or OpenAI, and any translation with Google Gemini, happen without Google Translate.

Full privacy policy: github.com/geryit/subly/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md

REQUIREMENTS

- Chrome 116 or later
- WebGPU recommended for best performance
- About 41-760 MB storage for a local model (downloaded on first use)
